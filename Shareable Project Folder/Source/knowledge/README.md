# Yard Coach knowledge and evaluation

The current engine is a deterministic knowledge and analytics system. No model weights were trained and no external inference service is used. Its behaviour is implemented in `shared/coach.mjs`, `coach-analysis.mjs` and `coach-languages.mjs`. The user's requested focus is the existing engine rather than a downloaded model.

`tradersyard.json` contains dated, source-linked summaries from the official company page and help centre. The Roman Urdu/Hindi explanations are in `roman-guidance.mjs`. Update the factual source and translations together when rules or staff change. Account-specific synced rules take priority over general guides; the public 1-Step evaluation article had ambiguous editorial wording, so the coach does not invent those missing thresholds.

Coverage includes company history/mission/team, plan rules, daily/EoD drawdown, consistency, profitable days, payouts, restrictions, news, overnight holding, allocation and risk-review status. Analytics cover all saved accounts: net P&L and fees, win/loss balance, observed break-even rate, realized drawdown, losing streaks, weakest accounts/markets, largest losses, tagged strategy comparisons, recent versus earlier history, missing risk and reviews. Different currencies are never added together. Correlation is not presented as the cause of a loss; missing trade notes cannot establish psychology, a violation or intent.

Language choices: English, Roman Urdu, Roman Hindi, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Chinese. Metric labels and the review plan have authored translations. English and Roman Urdu/Hindi include the most complete domain explanations; official knowledge excerpts and some technical explanations remain English in other choices. Auto-detection is heuristic, and the selector corrects ambiguous Roman Hindi/Urdu. These options do not establish universal language fluency or coverage of every question. Unknown topics receive an explicit bounded answer. A host-supplied language model through `adapter.coach` is needed for broad free-form reasoning and complete translation.

The acceptance tests in `tests/journal-v02.test.mjs` verify calculations, currency boundaries, source selection, empty journals, weak strategy samples, language routing and follow-up context. They are regression checks, not a multilingual model benchmark. Run `npm test` after editing knowledge or intent patterns. Have TradersYard's product/risk team approve business facts and native speakers review translations before production use.
